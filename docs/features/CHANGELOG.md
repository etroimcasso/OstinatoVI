# Feature Changelog

Chronological feature state transitions, newest first.

- **2026-10-27** — `battle-commands`: stays 🟡 in progress. Every command the player can choose runs (`runCommand()` in `src/battle/commands`): Fight with desperation attacks and Umaro's tackle, Storm and throw; Item, Throw and Tools, with the attack an item makes (`src/battle/item_attack`); Magic, X-Magic, Lore, Magitek, Summon, Slot, Health and Shock; Dance with its stumble; Morph, Revert, Row, Runic and Def; Steal, Capture, Sketch, Control, Leap, Possess and GP Rain; Rage; Bushido with Retort; Blitz; Jump with spears and the Dragon Horn; the Roulette command. Which commands can be chosen is refreshed by `refreshCommandMenu()` (`src/battle/command_menu`). A command's targets are taken back only while it is aimed at random. `BattleRng` gains `pickWeighted()`, shared with the choice of battle type; `loadMonsterTraits()` is the part of a monster a Rage takes on. `AttackAnimation` gains `item`, `chainSawKill`, `showsAttack` and `newBackground`; `BattleState` gains `holdingItem`; battle switch 65 is named. `docs/Bugs.md` gains the Umaro throw entry. The `battle-commands` target grows to 218 cases. C++ baseline now **1009/1017/8**; parser **1032/1032**.
